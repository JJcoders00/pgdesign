Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

In short: this website sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising, embeds no social media, and does not send anything you type to our servers. The only thing it may save in your browser is your sound on/off preference, and only if you turn the sound on.

1. Who we are

PG Design Interior Studio ("PG Design", "we"), BK Tower, SH40, Thodupuzha, Kerala 685584, India. Phone: +91 95444 49585. For the purposes of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 ("DPDP Act"), we are the Data Fiduciary for any personal data you choose to send us.

2. What this website collects

Nothing is collected or stored by this website itself. There is no account system, no newsletter, no tracking and no server-side form.

  • Contact form. When you fill in the form, the text is assembled inside your browser only. It is sent only if you then choose "Send via WhatsApp", "Send as SMS" or "Call instead". The message then travels through the app you chose (WhatsApp, which is operated by Meta Platforms, Inc., or your mobile carrier) under that provider's own privacy terms. What we receive is what you chose to send: your name, your phone number or email, and your message.
  • Sound preference. If you switch the background sound on, we save a single item named pg-sound in your browser's local storage so the choice is remembered on your next visit. It contains no identifier and is never sent anywhere. Switching sound off removes it; you can also clear it from your browser settings.

3. Third-party resources your browser loads

To display this page, your browser requests files from the following providers. When it does, your IP address and basic browser information are visible to them, as with any web request. We do not receive this information.

4. Hosting logs

Like almost every website, the server that hosts pgdesign.in may keep standard access logs (IP address, time, page requested, browser type) for security and fault-finding. These are kept by the hosting provider for the period set in its own policy and are not used by us to identify visitors.

5. Why we use your data and on what basis

We use the details you send us only to reply to your enquiry and, if you go ahead, to provide our design services. Under the DPDP Act this is based on your consent (you tick a box before preparing a message) and on data you voluntarily provide for a specified purpose (section 7(a)). For visitors in the EU/UK, the equivalent basis is your consent and our legitimate interest in answering you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and (f)).

6. How long we keep it

Enquiry messages are kept for as long as needed to answer you and, if you become a client, for the duration of the project and any period required by Indian tax and accounting law. If you do not become a client we delete your message when it is no longer needed, or sooner on request.

7. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. We do not share it with anyone except the communication providers you yourself chose to use (see section 2) and, where legally required, public authorities.

8. Your rights

India (DPDP Act 2023): you may ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you, have it corrected or erased, withdraw consent at any time, nominate another person to exercise your rights, and raise a grievance with us. If you are not satisfied with our response you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

European Economic Area and United Kingdom (GDPR / UK GDPR): you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. This website is directed at customers in India; we do not target EEA/UK residents, but if you contact us we will honour these rights.

California (CCPA/CPRA): we do not sell or share personal information and do not use it for cross-context behavioural advertising. You may request to know, delete or correct the information we hold.

Other regions: we respect the rights given by the privacy law that applies where you live.

To exercise any right, call us on +91 95444 49585 or visit the studio. We will respond within the time required by the applicable law and in any case within 30 days.

Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 3 October 2026

We want everyone to be able to use this website. It has been built with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 level AA as the target, which is also the standard referenced by India's Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016 and the Guidelines for Indian Government Websites.

What we have done

  • Every page element can be reached and operated with a keyboard, including the menu, image viewer, chapter controls and this dialog. A "Skip to content" link appears on first Tab press.
  • Text and background colours meet or exceed the AA contrast ratio of 4.5:1 for normal text.
  • Images carry text alternatives; purely decorative repeats are marked so screen readers skip them.
  • Form fields have visible labels, clear error messages that are announced, and a plainly labelled submit button.
  • Animation, smooth scrolling and background sound respect your system's "reduce motion" setting; sound is off by default and never autoplays.
  • The page works without JavaScript and remains readable on screens from 320 px wide upwards.

Known limitations

  • The studio's photographs are described generically because detailed captions were not available at the time of publishing. We will add fuller descriptions as they are written.
  • The "A Day" section is a scroll-driven presentation; if you find it difficult to use, the same photographs are available in the Frames section as a simple gallery.
